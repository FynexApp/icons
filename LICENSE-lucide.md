# Shapes derived from Lucide

The shapes of the icons in this package reuse the geometry of
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev), version 0.510.0. The animations, the factory,
the triggers and the split of each drawing into parts are the work of the
Fynex authors (MIT, see `LICENSE`). A few paths were rewritten in the other
direction, with an identical drawing, so that they draw from left to right
(`reversed` in `src/catalog.ts`).

A few shapes are not from Lucide: they are original Fynex drawings on the
same grid, under the MIT License like the rest of the package (`original` in
`src/catalog.ts`): `Krona`, `EuroArea`, `EarthEurope`, `EarthAmericas` and
`EarthAsia`.
